Metabolic Health & Glycemic Control
Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c)
3-month structural blood sugar average. Normal is <5.7%.
Fasting Glucose
Real-time serum blood sugar. Normal is 70-99 mg/dL.
Lipid Panel (Cardiovascular Fats)
LDL Cholesterol
The "Bad" cholesterol. Optimal is <100 mg/dL.
HDL Cholesterol
The "Good" protective cholesterol. Normal is >40 mg/dL.
Triglycerides
Stored fat lipids in serum. Normal is <150 mg/dL.
Complete Blood Count (CBC) - Blood Cells
White Blood Cell (WBC) Total
Immune response metrics. Standard line: 4.5 - 11.0 x10^3/uL.
Red Blood Cell (RBC) Total
Oxygen transport cells. Line baseline: 4.30 - 5.90 x10^6/uL.
Hemoglobin
Iron-based oxygen protein content. Line baseline: 13.5 - 17.5 g/dL.
Hematocrit
Volume percentage of red cells. Baseline line: 38.8% - 50.0%.
Platelet Count
Structural clotting cellular pieces. Baseline: 150 - 450 x10^3/uL.
