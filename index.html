Metabolic Health & Glycemic Control
Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c)
Approximate 2–3 month blood glucose marker. Normal: <5.7%.
Fasting Glucose
Blood glucose at the time of testing. Common fasting range: 70–100 mg/dL.
Lipid Panel
LDL Cholesterol
Low-density lipoprotein cholesterol. Common optimal target: <100 mg/dL.
HDL Cholesterol
High-density lipoprotein cholesterol. For adult men, ≥40 mg/dL is generally desirable.
Triglycerides
Circulating triglyceride concentration. Normal fasting level: <150 mg/dL.
Complete Blood Count (CBC)
White Blood Cell (WBC) Count
White blood cells involved in immune response. Adult reference: 4.5–11.0 ×10³/µL.
Red Blood Cell (RBC) Count
Red blood cells transport oxygen. Adult male reference: 4.6–6.2 ×10⁶/µL.
Hemoglobin
Oxygen-carrying protein in red blood cells. Adult male reference: 13–18 g/dL.
Hematocrit
Percentage of blood volume composed of red blood cells. Adult male reference: 40–55%.
Platelet Count
Platelets help blood clot. Adult reference: 150–400 ×10³/µL.
CMP — Kidney Function & Electrolytes
Creatinine
Waste product used in assessing kidney filtration. Common adult reference: 0.6–1.3 mg/dL.
BUN
Blood urea nitrogen. Common adult reference: 6–20 mg/dL.
Sodium
Major extracellular electrolyte. Common adult reference: 135–145 mEq/L.
Potassium
Electrolyte important for nerve, muscle and cardiac function. Common adult reference: 3.7–5.2 mEq/L.
Calcium
Serum calcium concentration. Common adult reference: 8.5–10.2 mg/dL.
CMP — Liver Markers
ALT
Alanine aminotransferase. Common adult reference: 4–36 U/L.
AST
Aspartate aminotransferase. Common adult reference: 8–33 U/L.
Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP)
Enzyme associated with liver, bile ducts and bone. Common adult reference: 20–130 U/L.
Total Bilirubin
Breakdown product formed during normal red-cell turnover. Common adult reference: 0.1–1.2 mg/dL.
Hepatitis Screening
Hepatitis C Antibody
Screening test for antibodies to hepatitis C virus. Expected screening result: nonreactive/negative.
